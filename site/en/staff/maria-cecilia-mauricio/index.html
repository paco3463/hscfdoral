María Cecilia Mauricio

Coach, consultant and therapist in communication, language, body awareness and emotions

Services
Coaching, Reflective yoga, Courses and workshops
Request an appointment with María Cecilia(305) 594-5658

Building coherence between what we think, feel, do and say.

Always searching for tools that help people communicate better and enjoy a better quality of life, I have trained as a speech therapist (fonoaudióloga), linguist, ontological coach, singing instructor, yoga instructor, Reiki practitioner and bio-emotional decoding practitioner, among other courses and degrees, with one goal: helping people express themselves physically, emotionally and spiritually.

For more than 17 years I have walked with children, families, adults and couples as they learn to be heard, be understood, reduce conflict, release emotions, move past blocks, relate better, find their best voice and build coherence between what they think, feel, do and say.

Areas of focus

  • Emotional expression
  • Reducing conflict
  • Voice and body expression
  • Couple and family relationships
