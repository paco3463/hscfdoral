Workshops and talks for parents

Our workshops and talks aim for a shift in perspective: understanding that children are teachers who invite their parents to pause, look at themselves and recognize themselves.

Request an appointment(305) 594-5658

Our goal is for parents to open up to different challenges from a place of compassion, acceptance and listening, and to discover what will lead each of them on a path of growth and change.

In the workshops we share strategies and tools that give parents and children the chance to succeed in a safe environment. Coaching helps children feel more prepared to face their challenges and learn how to handle them.
