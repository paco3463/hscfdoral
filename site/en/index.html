    What we do and how we can help

    What we do

    We work to improve the emotional, behavioral and social well-being of people of different ages and backgrounds: we promote mental health, help keep problems from starting or getting worse, and address behavior problems whenever possible.

    How we help

    Our mental health professionals are committed to quality care, guiding each person to face life effectively, manage stress and build positive relationships with others.

    ContactAbout us

    Our work

    Our workshops and talks aim for a shift in perspective: understanding that children are teachers who invite their parents to pause, look at themselves and recognize themselves.

    Our goal is for parents to open up to different challenges from a place of compassion, acceptance and listening, and to discover what will lead each of them on a path of growth and change. In the workshops we share strategies and tools that give parents and children the chance to succeed in a safe environment, and coaching helps children prepare to face their own challenges.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which insurance plans do you accept?

    We are in network with Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna and UnitedHealthcare, and we also work with Oscar and Bind. We do not accept TRICARE or plans affiliated with that network. Call us to confirm your coverage.

    Are there options if I don’t use insurance?

    Yes. We offer private mental health packages of 8, 12 or 16 weekly sessions, lasting two, three or four months.

    What evaluations do you offer?

    Evaluations for ages 2 to 21: developmental assessment (CSBS DP, ages 0 to 5), neuropsychological, autism, psychoeducational and gifted testing. We also offer psychological testing for adults.

    Do you offer therapy in Spanish?

    Yes. We are a bilingual team and see clients in Spanish and English. One of our therapists also works in Portuguese.

    Do you offer ABA therapy?

    Yes. We offer Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) to help build communication, social skills and independence, especially for children with autism and other developmental conditions.
