Our teen group focuses on self-improvement, self-esteem, depression, anxiety and improving relationships. This group will be limited to 3–5 teens.

Teen groups are an amazing way to support teens because they are at a developmental stage where connecting with each other becomes a priority. So, when we as therapists can create a safe space for teens to come together and truly connect, it becomes a powerful way to make positive changes and supercharge the healing process.
