Cogmed is an online training program designed to train working memory, created by leading neuroscientists to improve attention.

The program aims to help the person:

1. Improve their attention: resist distractions, plan activities, remember instructions, and start and finish tasks.
2. Improve their academic performance, specifically in math and reading comprehension.
3. Improve their performance at work and their ability to stay focused.

## How the program works

1. Structured interview
2. Start-up session
3. Weekly follow-up with the coach
4. Client access to the Cogmed training website

## Length

Cogmed sessions last 25 minutes, 2 to 3 times a week for 12 weeks.
